# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, startup founders, and potential clients (design leads, CTOs, early-stage teams) evaluating Rohith for senior in-house, contract, or freelance design roles. Secondary: design peers and the broader AI design community.

## Product Purpose

Rohith Reghu's personal portfolio. The site exists to demonstrate that his design work is intentional, technically grounded, and rooted in real outcomes — not aesthetic performance. Success means a prospect reads the work, trusts the craft, and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Intentional, technical, honest. Opinionated voice. Design that doesn't explain itself — it demonstrates.

## Anti-references

- Maximalist Dribbble-showcase portfolios: gradient explosions, floating 3D mockups, confetti animations that substitute motion for meaning.
- Ultra-minimal all-white Swiss grids that feel cold, generic, and interchangeable with any agency's site.
- Dark hacker aesthetic: neon on black, terminal cosplay, "AI designer" stereotypes.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** A designer who values purposeful design must build a site that is itself purposeful. Nothing decorative. Every element earns its place.
2. **Show, don't tell.** Layout, typography, and interaction quality demonstrate the designer's capability before the case studies even load. The site is the first proof of work.
3. **Technical honesty.** No gratuitous effects. No trendy flourishes applied without reason. The engineering depth shows in restraint, not complexity.
4. **Expert confidence.** Bold assertions, not hedged language. The interface expresses conviction, not approval-seeking.
5. **Human over heroic.** Warm and direct despite the technical depth. Not trying to impress with complexity — trying to be understood clearly.
